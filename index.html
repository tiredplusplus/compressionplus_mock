Image Compression App Mockup - Lin Guo

My app would have two adjacent image panels and a sliding compression bar at the top of the page. The upload button would be adjacent to the original image panel whereas the download button would be adjacent to the compressed image panel.

Users have the option to clear their current compressed image, add a new panel to compress the original image to a different k, or to change the original image without removing previous compression efforts.
